**Nergis Tomen:** I'm a faculty  member and co-director of the Biomorphic Intelligence Lab at TU Delft. My work is centered on biologically-inspired AI, neuromorphic computing and event-based vision for low-power applications. I am interested in performing ML research, where possible, with theoretical guarantees, through which I believe we can create more efficient and generalizable models. While numerous resources discuss the shortcomings of modern ML research, I believe this metascience initiative can bring together researchers from different disciplines and help form a collective to identify priorities as a community.
