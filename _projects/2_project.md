Our [second workshop](https://www.tudelft.nl/evenementen/2025/delft-ai/ellis/second-metascience-for-machine-learning-workshop) is on Oct 9, 2025 from 12:00-16:30 (walk in lunch); [register here](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=TVJuCSlpMECM04q0LeCIe_MHSpD9vMZLtuIwGfBrrkpUNjI4WVJGMVdZWUZXUTEwMEJERFVVRzNFVi4u) and has the following tentative schedule:

# Topic: Datasets and benchmarks

12:00 Walk in lunch at LB.01.040

12:35 Welcome in Snijderszaal

12:45 Joaquin Vanschoren

13:10 Martha Larson

13:35 Collective open reflection and discussion on the talks

14:15 Coffee break

14:45 Brief explanation/inventory of existing working groups and proposals of new ones; incentives people may need to put some serious effort into MS4ML

15:15 Continued break out discussions: the what, the how, and (most importantly) the time line per working group

16:00 Plenary update on plans for each group

16:15 Closing (announcements of final workshop and activities)
