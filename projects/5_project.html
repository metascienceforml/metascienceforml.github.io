Speaker presentations:

Session by Abigail Nieves.

In this interactive session, Abigail will introduce the history and philosophy of science followed by a group activity to discover the who, why, and what of Metascience for Machine Learning. If you have an opinion on what the challenges are in the field and why, how to make change etc, this is activity will formally facilitate that process through group discussion and identify concrete effective action.

Invited talk by David Tax.

Research into AI and Deep models / Machine Learning is growing rapidly, which puts strain on the usual scientific checks and reviews. As with any other scientific field, we want to start our investigation by measuring how relevant the issue is, and collect data which will help us analyze it. Here we focus on papers from the largest AI conference NeurIPS, and their own checklist which should correlate with the quality of a scientific paper. We are interested in seeing whether doing these checks using LLMs is trustworthy, and whether there are specific parts of the checklist the LLMs are bad at interpreting.This presentation reports on our first experience of investigating this topic with a BSc thesis project group in Q4 of the last academic year.
